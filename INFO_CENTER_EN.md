
# AuraGroove Info Center

**Application Version: 3.7.1**

## 1. About AuraGroove

AuraGroove is an autonomous musical intelligence that creates unique background music in real time. It is not an AI (LLM) and does not use text prompts. Every note is born "here and now" from mathematical algorithms.

## 2. 100% Offline Autonomy (Masterforge Vault)

The application is fully optimized for standalone operation.
* **Autonomy**: After synchronization via Masterforge Vault, the system works offline for up to 30 days.
* **Code Warm-up**: The Sync All function caches both audio atoms and the application code.
* **Persistence**: Installing as a PWA ensures "Persistent Storage" status in Chrome, protecting your data from automatic deletion.

## 3. Key Technologies

1. **Suite DNA Creation**: A harmonic and emotional plan is built from a single seed number.
2. **Velvet Standard**: Melodic ceiling at MIDI 71 for a warm, dense timbral profile.
3. **Fractal Mutations**: Algorithmic transformations of Heritage axioms (inversion, retrograde).

## 4. Your Privacy is Our Priority

We adhere to a **zero data collection** policy.
* **Zero Telemetry**: We do not collect usage statistics.
* **Local Storage Only**: All settings are stored exclusively on your device.
* **Offline-Native**: AuraGroove requires no server interaction after initial synchronization.
