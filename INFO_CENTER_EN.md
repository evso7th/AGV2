# AuraGroove Info Center

**Application Version: 0.4.12**

## 1. About AuraGroove

AuraGroove is an autonomous musical intelligence that creates unique background music in real time. Our system is not a neural network (like SUNO or Udio) and does not work with text prompts.

**It's a generator, not a player.** Every note is born "here and now" thanks to complex mathematical algorithms, rather than being played from a pre-made file. We simulate the performance of a live ensemble improvising for you within a given style.

**Our philosophy** is "mathematical purity." We create original, previously non-existent music that is perfect for concentration, work, or relaxation.

## 2. Key Technologies

Music in AuraGroove is born from a single number—the "Seed"—and goes through several stages of generation:

1.  **Creation of the Composition's "DNA":** Based on the seed, a harmonic and emotional plan for the entire piece is constructed.
2.  **Genre "Brain" Operation:** Specialized modules (`BluesBrain`, `AmbientBrain`, etc.) compose music according to the rules of the selected style.
3.  **Fractal Mutations:** Instead of simple repetition, the system uses mathematical mutations (inversion, retrograde) to create endless and organic variations.

For a deeper technical dive, please refer to the ["How Music is Born"](HOW_MUSIC_IS_BORN.md) document.

## 3. Your Privacy is Our Priority

We adhere to a **zero data collection** policy. This is not a marketing gimmick, but our fundamental principle.

*   **Zero Telemetry:** We do not collect usage statistics, analytics, or behavioral data.
*   **Zero Profiles:** We do not create or track user accounts or their activity history.
*   **No Personal Data:** The application does not request, access, or store your personally identifiable information.
*   **Local Storage Only:** All your settings—mixer presets, created routes—are stored exclusively on your device in your browser's `localStorage`. Nothing is ever sent to our servers.
*   **Offline-First:** AuraGroove can operate completely without internet access after the initial load.

## 4. Technical Recommendations and Limitations

### Voice Limit

The quality and richness of the sound directly depend on the number of simultaneously playing "voices."

*   **Recommendations:**
    *   **Modern PCs and Laptops:** 256-512 voices. This provides the highest sound quality.
    *   **Mobile Devices:** 64-128 voices. This is the optimal balance between quality and performance.
*   **How to Change:** The voice limit can be adjusted in the main navigator menu.

### Usage on Mobile Devices

Real-time music generation is a resource-intensive task. Please keep in mind:

*   **Interruptions:** Any incoming call or notification from another app can interrupt the audio. This is a limitation of mobile operating systems, not a bug in AuraGroove.
*   **Power Saving:** Aggressive battery-saving modes can "throttle" the application, leading to sound distortion.
*   **The Solution — Broadcast Bridge:** For an ideal listening experience on mobile devices, we strongly recommend using the "Broadcast Bridge" mode, which is detailed in the `USER_MANUAL_EN.md`. It allows your powerful device (PC) to do all the work, while your phone just plays the ready-made audio stream, which requires almost no resources.
