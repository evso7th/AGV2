
AuraGroove V 3.7.1 (Infinite Take Orchestra) – Complete User Guide
Welcome to AuraGroove V3—an autonomous musical intelligence that simulates the dynamics of a live ensemble.

1. Introduction: What is AuraGroove?
AuraGroove is a deterministic algorithmic engine that creates unique background music in real time using fractal mathematics, Markov chains, and genetic algorithms.

2. Personal Sound Setup
Sound in AuraGroove is synthesized in real time. Configure the System Mixer and Equalizer for your specific acoustic setup. Settings are saved locally.

3. Genres and "Brains"
* Slow Fusion (Ambient): Deep textures and complex harmonies.
* Cafe Blues: Expressive melodies and live improvisation.
* Neuro Space (Trance): Energetic rhythms and spiral structures.
* Dark Trance (Foundry): Industrial sound and heavy synthesis.
* Root Reggae: Riddim laws and swaying grooves.

4. 100% Eternal Offline Autonomy (Masterforge Vault)
As of v3.7.1, the application can function without internet for up to 30 days.
* Press "Sync All" in the Vault menu. The system caches all audio atoms and application code (Code Warm-up).
* Install as a PWA (Add to Home Screen) to prevent the browser from clearing your cache.
* Instant startup (Non-blocking DNA) ensures the engine plays immediately without network delays.

5. Genetic System
Each session is a unique organism. With "Heritage" enabled, the current seed is crossed with successful past sessions (masterpieces), inheriting their best traits.

6. Technical Recommendations
* Voice Limit (ARP): 50-120 voices recommended for mobile, up to 256 for desktop.
* Battery Saver: Mobile power-saving modes may distort audio. Use Broadcast Bridge for a stable experience.
* Velvet Standard: Melodic ceiling at MIDI 71 ensures warm and comfortable tonality.

Enjoy listening! Let mathematics create the perfect soundtrack for your day.
