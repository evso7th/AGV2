AuraGroove User Manual
Interface Version: v3.1 Stable | Core Engine: v3.7.0
Purpose: Algorithmic generative background music. No neural networks. No prompts. Zero data collection.
1. Introduction & Philosophy
AuraGroove (Infinite Take Orchestra) is not an AI music generator like SUNO or Udio. It is a deterministic music engine that creates unique, continuous soundscapes in real-time using fractal mathematics, Markov chains, and genetic algorithms.
Supported Genres: Ambient, Blues, Psytrance, Reggae.
Philosophy: "Press Play and Forget." Designed for background listening, focus, or relaxation. It requires no constant attention, never repeats exactly, and runs entirely locally after the initial load.

5. Performance Management (ARP: Voice Limit)
Click the CPU chip icon � in the bottom bar.
⚙️ Why is this needed?
The Adaptive Resource Protocol (ARP) controls the maximum number of simultaneously active voices (notes/instruments).
* Audio quality is directly proportional to voice count. More voices = richer, more detailed, "fuller" sound.
* Minimum: 50 voices. Experimentally determined threshold. Below this, sound loses polyphony, introducing artifacts and an "8-bit" character. The system blocks values below 50 to prevent digital noise.
* Maximum: 250 voices. More than sufficient to reproduce all musical nuances. Values above 250 provide no audible benefit but increase CPU load.
How to Adjust
1. Move the LIMIT slider to your desired value (50–250).
2. Press SAVE CHANGES.
3. � Important: If you change the limit during playback, press the Regenerate button () in the top bar to clear lingering voices and apply the new quota.
Recommendations:
* Desktop: 120–250
* Mobile: 60–120 (for stability)
* If experiencing stuttering: gradually lower the limit.
6. Sleep Timer
Click ⏱️ in the bottom bar.
* MINUTES Slider: Set session duration.
* ACTIVATE TIMER Button: Starts the countdown.
* Behavior: When the timer expires, music fades out smoothly over 8 bars, after which the session enters pause. AudioContext remains active for quick resumption.
7. Performance, Limitations & Mobile Devices
⚠️ Critical for Smartphones
AuraGroove is a web app generating sound in real-time. It cannot control operating system resource management.
* Calls & Notifications: Any incoming call will immediately stop audio playback. This is an OS limitation, not an app bug.
* Resource Contention: Opening another app steals CPU/memory → may cause artifacts or quality drops.
* Battery Saver: Low battery triggers aggressive OS throttling → distortion or complete shutdown.
* Background Mode: iOS Safari throttles timers in the background. For stable playback, keep the tab active or use Picture-in-Picture mode.
Recommendation: For extended listening, use a dedicated device, keep it plugged in, and close background apps. Minimum specs: ~500k AnTuTu, 2GB RAM.

9. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
❓ Can I use the generated music in commercial projects?
No. The CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license permits non-commercial use only. For commercial inquiries, contact the developer directly.
❓ Why does audio stop when I receive a call on mobile?
This is a fundamental mobile OS architecture limitation. Voice calls always take priority over web audio. No application can override this system behavior.
❓ How can I share a generated fragment?
Use the built-in recording (WebM) or share function. Note that exact reproduction of the same session is impossible due to genetic mutations—this is intentional, part of the "infinite take" concept.
❓ Does it work offline?
Yes. After initialization, the engine generates music entirely locally. Cloud synchronization is optional and only used for updating the genetic pool.
