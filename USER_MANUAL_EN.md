# AuraGroove User Manual

**Document Version: 2.0**

## 1. Welcome to AuraGroove!

AuraGroove is not just a player; it's an autonomous musical intelligence that creates unique music in real time. Unlike AI networks like SUNO/Udio, AuraGroove doesn't use text prompts. Instead, it generates every note based on complex mathematical models and genetic algorithms, simulating the performance of a live musical ensemble.

This guide will help you master all the system's features, from the initial sound setup to creating your own musical journeys.

## 2. First Things First: Your Personal Sound Check

Every device (phone, laptop) and every audio system (headphones, speakers) sounds different. To get the best experience, the first step is to calibrate the sound for yourself. **You only need to do this once.**

1.  **Press the "Play" button** to start the music.
2.  **Open the "Grand Studio Console"**: Click on the icon with three sliders (<SlidersHorizontal />) in the top-right corner.
3.  **Adjust the Mixer:**
    *   You will see a set of vertical faders for each instrument part (Master, Bass, Melody, etc.).
    *   By moving the faders up and down, adjust the volume of each part to your liking. Make the bass louder, the melody softer—achieve the perfect balance for you.
4.  **Open the Equalizer (EQ):**
    *   Close the console and click the "EQ" button.
    *   The equalizer allows you to boost or cut specific frequencies. If the sound seems "muffled," slightly raise the high frequencies (2k, 4k). If it's "boomy," try slightly lowering the low frequencies (125, 250).
5.  **Save Your Settings as a Preset:**
    *   A detailed description of working with presets is in the next section, but the main principle is to save your basic mix and EQ so you don't have to configure them every time.

Now the system is calibrated to your acoustics, and you are ready for a deeper dive.

## 3. Managing Mixer and EQ Presets

Presets are a powerful tool for saving and quickly recalling your favorite sound settings.

### 3.1. Saving and Updating a Preset

*   **To save a preset:** After you've configured the mixer or equalizer, you need to open the corresponding section in the navigation panel (available on the main navigator screen), enter a name for the preset (e.g., "My Sony Headphones"), and press "Save".
*   **To update a preset:** If you've made changes to an already loaded preset, simply press the "Update Active Preset" button. The settings will be overwritten.

### 3.2. Linking a Preset to a Genre

This is a key feature for automation. Different music genres often require different mixer settings. For example, Reggae needs a loud, prominent bass, while Ambient requires a soft, enveloping one.

1.  **Load the preset** you want to link.
2.  In your list of presets, **select a genre** from the dropdown menu next to the preset name.

Now, when you switch genres in your Playback Queue (Route), AuraGroove will **automatically** load the corresponding mix and EQ. If no preset is assigned to a genre, the system's default settings will be used.

## 4. Key Features and Controls

### 4.1. Main Buttons

*   **Play/Pause:** Starts and stops playback. The first time you start, it may take a few seconds to initialize the audio engine.
*   **Regenerate (Button with a circular arrow):** If you don't like the current musical idea, press this button. The system will instantly create a new "Seed" and generate a completely new composition within the selected genre and mood. It's like asking the ensemble, "Folks, let's try something different."
*   **Save Masterpiece (The "Like" button):** If you hear something brilliant, press "Like." This saves the "DNA" (seed, settings) of this composition to the cloud library of "masterpieces." In the future, with the "Heritage" feature enabled, other users (and yourself) can generate new music genetically inherited from this successful variant.

### 4.2. Recording Modes

AuraGroove offers two recording modes:

1.  **Manual Recording (The <Radio /> button):**
    *   **What it does:** Records the music to a `.webm` file on your device.
    *   **How to use:** Press once to start recording. Press a second time to stop and save the file. Ideal for recording short, favorite snippets.

2.  **Album Mode (Full Track Record):**
    *   **What it does:** Automatically records an entire musical piece from start to finish.
    *   **How to use:** Go to the navigator, select "Full Track Record" mode. The system will start playback, record the entire composition (e.g., 160 bars), and **automatically stop recording** when the piece logically concludes. This is the best way to get a perfectly recorded, finished track.

### 4.3. Broadcast Bridge: Music for Low-End Devices

*   **The Problem:** Music generation requires significant processor resources. On older phones or when running other tasks, this can cause stuttering and audio artifacts.
*   **The Solution — Broadcast Bridge (The <TowerControl /> button):** This is a special mode that distributes the load.
    *   You open AuraGroove on a **powerful device** (like your PC or laptop) and activate "Broadcast" mode. This device becomes your "radio station."
    *   Then, you open AuraGroove on a **low-end device** (like your old phone) and also press "Broadcast."
    *   **What happens:** The low-end device no longer generates music. It simply receives the ready-made, compressed audio stream from your "radio station" over the local network. This requires almost no processor resources and ensures crystal-clear sound without glitches.
*   **Why it's needed:** To listen to high-quality music on any device, even the weakest ones, or to ensure uninterrupted playback on your main phone without fear of an incoming call or another app interrupting the music.

### 4.4. Creating a Playback Queue (Route / Journey)

You can become the DJ of your own musical journey.

1.  On the main navigator screen, find the "Current Route" section.
2.  Select a **Genre** and a **Mood**.
3.  Press **"Add to Route"**. The pair will be added to your playlist.
4.  You can add as many tracks as you like, shuffle them, and save or load entire "journeys."
5.  When you press Play, AuraGroove will automatically switch between the tracks in your playlist, creating a seamless musical experience.

### 4.5. "DNA Selection" and Heritage

*   **Heritage:** This is a toggle that activates the genetic algorithms. When enabled, new music is not created from a purely random "seed," but by "crossbreeding" with the DNA of saved "masterpieces."
*   **DNA Selection (The "DNA Anchor" / "DNA Hybrid" button):** This window allows you to manually select which "masterpieces" from the cloud library will be used for crossbreeding. You can choose one "anchor" to make all the music similar to it, or several to create a "hybrid" of different styles. This gives you the deepest control over the genetic code of your music.
