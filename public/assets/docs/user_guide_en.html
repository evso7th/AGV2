AuraGroove V 0.4.50: Complete User Guide

1. Welcome to AuraGroove!

AuraGroove is an autonomous musical intelligence that creates unique music in real time. Unlike AI networks like SUNO/Udio, it doesn't use text prompts. Instead, it generates every note based on complex mathematical models and genetic algorithms, simulating a live ensemble performance on your device.

2. Personal Sound Setup

Every device sounds different. Calibrate the system for your acoustics (you only need to do this once):

  • Press Play.
  • Open the Grand Studio Console (sliders icon).
  • Adjust volumes for Bass, Lead, and Keyboards to reach your perfect balance.
  • Use the EQ to boost or cut frequencies to match your headphones or speakers.

3. Managing Presets

Presets store your favorite settings. After configuring the mixer, enter a name in the navigator and press "Save". Presets are stored locally on your device.

4. Linking Presets to Genres

Different genres need different balances. Select a genre from the dropdown next to a saved preset. AuraGroove will then automatically load that mix when you switch genres in your Route.

5. Main Controls

  • Play/Pause: Starts and stops playback.
  • Regenerate: Creates a new "Seed" and a completely new composition within the same genre/mood.
  • Save Masterpiece (Like): Saves the composition's "DNA" to the cloud for future inheritance.

6. Recording Modes

Manual: Short tap on Radio icon to record a snippet. Full Track Record (FTR): Long press (1.5s) to record an entire 160-bar piece automatically.

7. Broadcast Bridge

A specialized mode for low-end devices. A powerful device (PC) acts as a station generating the music, while your mobile device simply plays the audio stream to prevent stuttering.

8. Creating a Journey (Route)

Select Genre and Mood, then tap "Add to Route". You can build playlists, shuffle them, or save entire journeys to your local library.

9. DNA & Heritage

Heritage toggle activates genetic memory. New music is created by crossbreeding with saved "masterpieces". Anchor mode lets you lock one track as the exclusive genetic donor.

10. Voice Limit (ARP)

Balance quality and performance. Suggested: 256-512 for PC, 50-120 for mobile. Adjust via the CPU chip icon in the controls.

11. Privacy & Offline Use

Zero data collection policy. No tracking, no profiles. AuraGroove works completely offline after the initial asset load.
